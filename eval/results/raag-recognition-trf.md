# Raag recognition on real recordings

Dataset: Thaat and Raga Forest (Kaggle / IEEE DataPort, AGPL-3.0), a fixed sample: the first two recordings (by file number, under 40 MB) of each of the 48 raags whose folder names map to one raag in this app, 96 recordings. Concert, film and instrumental recordings with accompaniment; up to 5 minutes from 30 s in. No Sa labels, so only automatic Sa is measured. The app chooses among all 125 raags in its library.
Generated by `npm run eval:raag`.

| Sa | Top 1 | Top 3 | Top 1 up to same swaras | First guess in the right thaat | Median rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Automatic (found from the recording, raag unknown) | 16 / 96 (17%) | 22 / 96 (23%) | 22 / 96 (23%) | 26 / 96 (27%) | 31 |
