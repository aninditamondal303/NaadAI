## Raag Yaman (Kalyan)

- [ ] Thaat: Kalyan
- [ ] Jati: Sampurna-Sampurna
- [ ] Time: Evening (6 PM - 9 PM)
- [ ] Swaras used: S (Sa), R (Re), G (Ga), M (tivra Ma), P (Pa), D (Dha), N (Ni)
- [ ] Not used (varjit): none
- [ ] Aroh: N, R G M D N S'
- [ ] Avaroh: S' N D P M G R S
- [ ] Vadi: G · Samvadi: N
- [ ] Mood: Devotional, peaceful, serene, romantic
- [ ] Pakad: N, R G R | P R | N, R S
- [ ] Chalan 1: N, R G | R G M P
- [ ] Chalan 2: M D N S'
- [ ] Chalan 3: S' N D P | M G R S
- [ ] Chalan 4: P R | N, R S
- [ ] Nyas swaras: G, P, N
- [ ] Note: Uses tivra Ma. The aroh usually starts from mandra Ni (N, R G) rather than from Sa.
- [ ] Compared with Shuddha Kalyan: Shuddha Kalyan ascends like Bhupali (S R G P D S'), leaving out Ma and Ni, and uses them only lightly in descent. Yaman uses tivra Ma and Ni freely in both directions.
- [ ] Common mistake: Touching shuddha Ma instead of tivra Ma (shuddha Ma belongs to Yaman Kalyan, not Yaman).
- [ ] Common mistake: Starting the ascent from Sa with S R G instead of the characteristic N, R G.

## Raag Bhupali

- [ ] Thaat: Kalyan
- [ ] Jati: Audav-Audav
- [ ] Time: First Part of Night (7 PM - 10 PM)
- [ ] Swaras used: S (Sa), R (Re), G (Ga), P (Pa), D (Dha)
- [ ] Not used (varjit): Ma, Ni
- [ ] Aroh: S R G P D S'
- [ ] Avaroh: S' D P G R S
- [ ] Vadi: G · Samvadi: D
- [ ] Mood: Pure joy, pristine clarity, deep resonance
- [ ] Pakad: G R S D, | S R G | P G | D P G R S
- [ ] Chalan 1: S R G | P G | D P G R S
- [ ] Chalan 2: G P D S'
- [ ] Chalan 3: S' D P G R S
- [ ] Nyas swaras: G, P, S
- [ ] Note: Pentatonic: Ma and Ni are omitted (varjit).
- [ ] Compared with Deshkar: Same five swaras, but Deshkar (Bilawal thaat, a morning raag) centres on Dha and Pa in the upper half and avoids Bhupali's G R S, D movement. Bhupali (evening) dwells on Ga and Re.
- [ ] Compared with Shuddha Kalyan: Shuddha Kalyan has Bhupali's ascent but brings tivra Ma and Ni into the descent.
- [ ] Common mistake: Letting Ma or Ni slip into descending phrases.
- [ ] Common mistake: Treating Dha as the main resting note, which drifts towards Deshkar.

## Raag Bhairav

- [ ] Thaat: Bhairav
- [ ] Jati: Sampurna-Sampurna
- [ ] Time: Morning (4 AM - 6 AM)
- [ ] Swaras used: S (Sa), r (komal Re), G (Ga), m (Ma), P (Pa), d (komal Dha), N (Ni)
- [ ] Not used (varjit): none
- [ ] Aroh: S r G m P d N S'
- [ ] Avaroh: S' N d P m G r S
- [ ] Vadi: d · Samvadi: r
- [ ] Mood: Solemn, meditative, prayerful, divine
- [ ] Pakad: S | G m d d P | G m r r S
- [ ] Chalan 1: S r G m P
- [ ] Chalan 2: G m d d P
- [ ] Chalan 3: m P d N S'
- [ ] Chalan 4: S' N d P | m G r S
- [ ] Nyas swaras: m, P, S
- [ ] Note: Komal Re and komal Dha are sung with a slow andolan (gentle oscillation).
- [ ] Compared with Kalingada: Same swaras, but Kalingada is lighter: komal Re and Dha are sung without Bhairav's slow andolan, and it moves more freely.
- [ ] Compared with Ramkali: Ramkali adds tivra Ma and komal Ni in descending phrases (e.g. M P d n d P).
- [ ] Common mistake: Holding komal Re or komal Dha flat and still, without the andolan.
- [ ] Common mistake: Rushing: Bhairav is usually developed slowly.

## Raag Miya ki Todi

- [ ] Thaat: Todi
- [ ] Jati: Sampurna-Sampurna
- [ ] Time: Morning (9 AM - 12 PM)

- [ ] Not used (varjit): none
- [ ] Aroh: S r g M d N S'
- [ ] Avaroh: S' N d P M g r S
- [ ] Vadi: d · Samvadi: g
- [ ] Mood: Melancholy, supreme spiritual yearning
- [ ] Pakad: d, N, S r g | r g r S
- [ ] Chalan 1: S r g | M d
- [ ] Chalan 2: M d N S'
- [ ] Chalan 3: S' N d | M g r g r S
- [ ] Nyas swaras: g, d, S
- [ ] Note: Komal Re, komal Ga and komal Dha are sung low and with care; Pa is used sparingly (often skipped in the ascent).
- [ ] Compared with Multani: Same scale, but Multani uses komal Re and Dha only weakly, rests on Pa and komal Ga, and starts phrases from mandra Ni (N, S g M P). Multani is an afternoon raag.
- [ ] Compared with Bilaskhani Todi: Bilaskhani Todi belongs to Bhairavi thaat (shuddha Ma, komal Ni) and has an avaroh that moves through Pa unusually.
- [ ] Common mistake: Singing komal Re and Ga too high.
- [ ] Common mistake: Resting on Pa, which drifts towards Multani.
